Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to some of the most common questions about LearnHub.

How Can We Help You?

Browse the questions below to find the information you're looking for.

What is LearnHub?

LearnHub is an online learning platform where students can discover courses, learn new skills, and improve their knowledge through online education.

How do I create an account?

Click on the Register option in the navigation menu, enter your name, email address, and password, and submit the registration form.

How do I log in?

Click on the Login option and enter the email address and password you used during registration.

How can I find a course?

Go to the Courses page to browse all available courses. You can also search for courses or filter them by category, level, price, and rating.

Can I add a course to my cart?

Yes. Open the course details page and click the "Add to Cart" button. You can then view the course from your shopping cart.

How do I purchase a course?

Add the course to your cart, open the cart page, and continue to checkout. Enter the required information and complete the payment process.

What payment methods are available?

LearnHub can support different payment methods such as credit cards, debit cards, UPI, and other online payment options.

Where can I find my purchased courses?

After successfully purchasing a course, you can find it in the "My Courses" section of your account.

Can I save courses for later?

Yes. You can add courses to your Wishlist and access them later without adding them to your cart.

Will I receive a certificate?

Courses can provide a certificate after you successfully complete the required lessons, quizzes, or final assessment.

Can I learn at my own pace?

Yes. You can access your enrolled courses and continue learning according to your own schedule.

What should I do if I have another question?

If you cannot find the answer you're looking for, visit our Contact page and send us a message.
